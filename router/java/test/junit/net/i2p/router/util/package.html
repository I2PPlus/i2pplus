The router's own data structures. The cached-iterator pair is covered for the contract that makes them cheap: the iterator is reused, and the view reflects the collection as it changes. The decaying filters - {@code DecayingBloomFilter} and {@code DecayingHashSet} - are covered for entry and lookup semantics, including that {@code add()} reports "already present" rather than "newly added", and separately for the sizing accessors and the run-time cap setter. {@code PeerLogFormatterTest} pins the peer sub-list layout the selectors emit: the label line, the three-space indent, at most eight hashes per line, and the "more" tail. Run by ant test in router/java.
