The floodfill netdb's decisions, tested as pure statics rather than through a running router. The routing table: picking the closest top-K peers, the bucket size, evicting the entry with the oldest contact evidence past the grace window, the debug table's one-row-per-bucket rendering, and the transient store's expiry. Searching and flooding: the iterative search, the flood throttler, the masked-IP key a floodfill peer selector groups by, and the negative lookup cache - repeated failures accumulate toward the cap, so one transient search failure must not poison a router's key and cut off transit through it.

Lease sets and lifetimes: reminting a router info, replacing a local lease set, publishing and republishing it and the log a republish emits, the local lease set's fallback, expiring routers, draining the refresh job, probing a stale peer's score, the introducer cap and introducer lookup, peer verification, the floodfill verify decision, and the ban decision out of a netdb validation failure.
