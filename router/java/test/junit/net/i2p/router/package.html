The router's own machinery, tested as decisions rather than through a running router. The autotuner is the bulk of it: the parameter keys, the bounds a callee may apply, the direction a ceiling moves in, the targets for IO transfer, lookup limit, reflector, search budget, send window and stall timeout, the health weights, signal freshness, the activity window, the observed-stat context, the static overrides, the thread-CPU table, and the invariants that must hold across all of them.

Beside it: the banlist (capability patterns, entry age, policy decisions) and the blocklist; the {@code JobQueue} (in-flight accounting, its index, the lease a job holds, pumper notification, rescheduling, and the scaler's decision and feedback); {@code MessageValidator}; the transports a failed outbound message reports; the streaming ceiling decision; the build-reply timeout range; and tunnel test status. {@code RouterTestHelper} is a fixture, not a suite. Run by {@code ant test} in {@code router/java}.
