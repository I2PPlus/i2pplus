SSU and SSU2, tested as decisions rather than through a socket. The peer state: the eligibility decisions, congestion, inbound eviction, the effective retransmission timeout as the clamp of the raw estimate, and the peer test manager's retransmit decision. Establishment: the decision, its timeout floor and slow-establish threshold, the idle-established decision, peer expiry, the outbound establish expiry, the inbound reschedule that must not outrun its own deadline, and how a corrupt confirm, an inbound giveup, and a log line are reported. The wire format: the MTU, the packet builder, outbound message fragments, the SSU2 bitfield - where an attacker-controlled ACK-block span is bounded before any bitfield is allocated - the payload block, the post-quantum handshake's sizes and message staging, and {@code SSU2Util}.

Beside them: the endpoint, the IP throttler, the reachability and address update and bid decisions, and {@code UDPFlooder}, a manual fixture that sends random data to every UDP peer at a set rate - not a suite. Run by ant test in router/java.
