Peer profiling and selection, tested as pure decisions rather than through a running router. The bulk is the {@code ProfileOrganizer}: candidate eligibility and sampling, the cap on profiles, the active window, the ban filter, subnet diversity, self-exclusion, the RTT ceiling, the selection cost, the speed-rank and general decision paths, promotion and tier admission with its floor and shortfall, the tier union, restore and purge (including the purge lock), the refresh decision, strike counting, and the neutral build-success value when there are no stats.

Beside it: {@code PeerProfile} itself, with the peak decay; {@code ProfileManagerImpl} for floodfill reliability and demotion scope, with the {@code FloodfillReliability} ordering; {@code TunnelHistory}; the latency machinery - the DB-history metric, first-hop RTT, the protocol RTT baseline, measurement fidelity, and the calibrated low-latency flag; the profile retention and lossy-readmit decisions; the peer-selection bound; and the router-info refresher's bound. Run by ant test in router/java.
