The post-quantum key encapsulation mechanism the handshake uses, piece by piece: the centered-binomial noise sampler at eta 2 and eta 3, including the all-zero and all-ones byte inputs; the number-theoretic transform and its constants; polynomial and polynomial-vector arithmetic; the modular reduction; and the symmetric layer (the SHAKE-derived generation and the PRF) with its byte sizes. {@code MLKEMEngineTest} covers the engine over the K-2, K-3 and K-4 parameter sets - the key, ciphertext and shared-secret byte counts, an invalid parameter set, and the encapsulation/decapsulation round trip. Run by ant test in router/java.
