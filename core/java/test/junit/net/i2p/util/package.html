The core utilities: the encodings ({@code Base32} / {@code Base64} are in the data suite; here {@code Escapes}, {@code HexDump}, {@code Addresses}, {@code ConvertToHash} and {@code RFC822Date}); the collections and caches ({@code ArraySet}, {@code ConcurrentHashSet}, {@code LHMCache}, {@code TryCache}, {@code SimpleByteCache}, {@code ObjectCounter}, {@code OrderedProperties}); the streams (the resettable and reusable GZIP pairs, {@code LookAheadInputStream}, {@code SecureFileOutputStream}); and the files ({@code FileUtil.rmdir}, {@code SecureDirectory} permissions).

The timing and scheduling machinery is tested here too: {@code SimpleTimer2} for scaling, thread count, stall cadence, and the watchdog and work-due decisions; {@code StallRegistry}; {@code SyntheticREDQueue}; the {@code SystemVersion} load caches; {@code FortunaRandomSource}; {@code PasswordManager}; {@code EepGet} for its failure reasons and its usage output; {@code LogSettings}, {@code LogRecordFormatter} and the log-suppressor decision; {@code NativeBigInteger} family detection; {@code VersionComparator}; and the build-success ratio. {@code UtilTestSuite} gathers the group; {@code ant test} in core/java runs each {@code *Test.java}.
