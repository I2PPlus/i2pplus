The curve arithmetic under Ed25519: the field constants, the group element operations, the utility math, and the precomputation tables checked against their published vectors. {@code AbstractFieldElementTest} holds the cases every field-element representation must pass.

The build runs {@code *Test.java} by pattern and excludes {@code AbstractFieldElementTest} from that run, since it is the shared base, not a suite of its own; {@code MathUtils} is a fixture, not a test.
