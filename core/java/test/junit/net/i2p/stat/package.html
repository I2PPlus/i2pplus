The statistics subsystem: {@code Rate} and {@code RateStat} themselves, and the coalescing that keeps them cheap - the isolation of one period's samples, the skipping of empty ones, the step between periods, the heartbeat that closes a period, who owns a listener, which samples count as recent, and how a listener is delivered to. {@code StatManager} is covered for the coalesce heartbeat and the rate handle it hands out.

{@code SimpleStatDumper} and {@code SizeMeasure} are fixtures, not suites: the first prints a stat tree for a test to assert on, the second measures the footprint of an object. {@code StatTestSuite} gathers the group; {@code ant test} in core/java runs each {@code *Test.java}.
