The streaming library, tested as decisions rather than over a socket. The connection's congestion and loss response: slow-start threshold and its bandwidth anchor, congestion-avoidance window growth, the window ceiling, the throughput floor and its baseline policy, choke and unchoke with their size sampling, graduated loss response, the retransmit delay and count, the hard resend budget, the stall give-up budget and its verdict on outstanding data, the soft-failure reschedule, recovery, the RTT and its initial default, and the log line a connection emits.

The handshake: the SYN timeout, expiry, reaper and its rate, the SYN-ACK resend decision and the SYN budget and retransmit interval, accept timeout resolution, the worker, and the throttler and cooldown wait that keep a flood of connects off the pool. The manager: its decisions, ping, pool pruning, reject response, and shutdown. The packet layer: the dispatcher and its retired-session hand-off, serialization, the signature layout, ingress hardening, local ack starvation and RTT sampling, and the payload release and its race. The streams: input and output, the buffer decay, the NACK cap, output-stream destroy, and the standard socket's contract.

Beside them: the option loading from the cached config and its RTO, RTT, resend-delay order, max connect timeout and sample clamp; the scheduler's dead-task decision; the live-stream stats cadence, send-size telemetry and retransmit-ratio stat; stream reservation; the temporary-ban decision; the duplicate-close limiter; field visibility for the peer's own fields; and the rate limit on a repeated warning. {@code TaskSchedulerTestBase} is the shared base, not a suite. Run by ant test in apps/streaming/java.
